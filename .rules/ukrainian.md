# Ukrainian Contrastive Phonetic Phenomena (`Українська`)

- **Palatalization (Hard vs. Soft consonants):** Dental/alveolar consonants contrast between non-palatalized ("hard") and palatalized ("soft") counterparts (e.g., лук `/ɫuk/` 'bow/onion' vs. люк `/lʲuk/` 'hatch', or лан `/ɫɑn/` 'field' vs. лань `/ɫɑnʲ/` 'doe').
- **Consonant Gemination (Length):** Consonants contrast between single (short) and geminate (long) length (e.g., рілля `/rʲilʲˈːɑ/` 'arable land' vs. ріля `/rʲiˈlʲɑ/` 'hurdy-gurdy', or знання `/znɑˈnʲːɑ/` 'knowledge').
- **Lexical Stress:** Stress is free and phonemically contrastive, distinguishing word meanings and grammatical forms (e.g., за́мок `/ˈzɑmɔk/` 'castle' vs. замо́к `/zɑˈmɔk/` 'lock', or му́ка `/ˈmukɑ/` 'torment' vs. мука́ `/muˈkɑ/` 'flour').
