# Bengali Contrastive Phonetic Phenomena (`বাংলা`)

- **Aspiration & Voicing (Four-way stop contrast):** Stops contrast across voiceless unaspirated, voiceless aspirated, voiced unaspirated, and voiced aspirated (breathy) series (e.g., ক `/k/`, খ `/kʰ/`, গ `/ɡ/`, ঘ `/ɡʱ/` — কাল `/kal/` 'tomorrow/yesterday' vs. খাল `/kʰal/` 'canal' vs. গাল `/ɡal/` 'cheek').
- **Vowel Nasalization:** All seven oral vowels contrast phonemically with their nasalized counterparts marked by *chandrabindu* (`ঁ`) (e.g., কাদা `/kad̪a/` 'mud' vs. কাঁদা `/kãd̪a/` 'to weep').
- **Place of Articulation (Dental vs. Retroflex):** Coronal stops contrast between dental and retroflex places of articulation (e.g., ত `/t̪/`, থ `/t̪ʰ/`, দ `/d̪/`, ধ `/d̪ʱ/` vs. ট `/ʈ/`, ঠ `/ʈʰ/`, ড `/ɖ/`, ঢ `/ɖʱ/` — সাত `/ʃat̪/` 'seven' vs. ষাট `/ʃaʈ/` 'sixty').
